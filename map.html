Map

India is a vast and diverse country located in South Asia, known for its rich geographical features and cultural diversity. It is the seventh-largest country in the world and shares its borders with countries like Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar.The map of India represents its 28 states and 8 Union Territories, each with its own unique identity, language, and traditions. From the snow-covered Himalayan mountains in the north to the coastal plains in the south, the map highlights the incredible physical diversity of the nation. Major rivers such as the Ganga, Yamuna, and Brahmaputra flow across the country, supporting agriculture and livelihoods. The map also shows important cities, capitals, and neighboring countries, helping us understand India’s position on the global stage.Studying the map of India not only helps us learn about geography but also strengthens our sense of unity and national identity. It reminds us how diverse regions come together to form one nation — India
